## What the Carbon Capture Commit Data Tells Us for the Podcast

1. **OpenAir-Cyan is the hero narrative AND the cautionary tale.** It proved DIY open-source hardware can get certified. It also proved that without institutional support, even certified hardware goes dormant.

2. **The August 2026 materials wave is the biggest story.** 4+ repos updating in 6 days, with CC0 public domain licensing. Computational sorbent screening is becoming reproducible and open. This is where the field is moving.

3. **Literature surveys dominate because the field isn't tool-ready.** 56 stars for a bibliography repo vs. 2 stars for a working DAC materials screening code. The carbon capture community values knowledge integration over tool-building.

4. **Stars ≠ maintenance.** 85 stars for a ghost repo vs. 2 stars for active code. The academic incentive structure rewards citation counts, not code sustainability.

5. **CC0 licensing is the open-science revolution.** tjz21's decision to dedicate computational screening data to the public domain (CC0) is the most significant open-science finding in the entire analysis. This is how you build public infrastructure.

6. **The engineering layer is thin.** Only one repo (CO2-Soft-Sensor) builds a practical plant-control model. Materials science is active; system engineering is absent.
