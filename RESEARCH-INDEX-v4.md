## The Three Themes at a Glance

### ☀️ Solar Geoengineering (solar-geoengineering branch)
- **Top repo:** WRF (1,761 stars) — atmospheric model with SRM-relevant physics changes in v4.8.0
- **Best economic tool:** ClimateMARGO.jl (73 stars) — dormant but showing revival signal
- **Most active niche:** awesome-geoengineering (4 stars) — accelerating curation
- **Biggest finding:** WRF disabled aerosol-aware schemes in v4.8.0 — a silent shift in SRM simulation capability
- **Podcast hook:** "The world's best climate model just turned off its aerosol mode"

### 🌍 Carbon Capture (carbon-capture branch)
- **Top ecosystem:** open-sustainable-technology (2,552 stars) — continuously maintained directory
- **Only DIY DAC:** OpenAir-Cyan (76 stars) — OSHWA-certified, then frozen
- **CC0 revolution:** tjz21's DAC peroxovanadate/peroxotitanate repos — public domain sorbent data
- **New narrative:** CarbonLens (LCA) and OpenCarbon (clean-energy-for-DAC) — "should we capture?" not just "can we?"
- **Biggest finding:** The only open-source DAC hardware froze after certification
- **Podcast hook:** "The only DIY DAC you can build froze right after it got certified"

### 🌊 Ocean Intervention (ocean-intervention branch)
- **Zero repos found** — the largest gap in open-source climate tech
- **Closest tool:** MDTF-diagnostics precip-buoyancy POD (5 commits in 1 day, diagnostic only)
- **Adjacent physics:** Oceananigans.jl (1,413 stars) — ocean simulation, no interventions
- **Four hypotheses for the void:** Funding, governance taboo, technical difficulty, public good problem
- **Biggest finding:** The GitHub vacuum is a governance signal
- **Podcast hook:** "The ocean is the forbidden quadrant — solar gets models, carbon gets hardware, ocean gets nothing"
