## 🚨 Headline Finding: The Ocean Intervention Gap

Our GitHub search across multiple query strategies returned **zero dedicated ocean geoengineering repositories.** This is the most significant finding of the entire research effort. Ocean geoengineering is the "dark matter" of climate tech on GitHub — it exists in the scientific literature but not in open code.

**See:** [ocean-intervention/README.md](https://github.com/bro26man-hash/climate-pod-research/blob/ocean-intervention/README.md)

## 📊 Commit Trend Highlights (Updated Sep 2026)

### What the commit histories tell us:

1. **Institutional bursts dominate climate software** — PCMDI's 133★ had 10 commits in 2 days (v4.2.1 release, Sep 3-4, 2026). All commits by Jiwoo Lee at LLNL with PRs from James Goodnight and Jared Lewis. Most repos can't sustain this.

2. **The August 2026 DAC materials wave** — Three repositories (peroxovanadates, peroxotitanates, electro-swing DAC) were all active on Aug 19, 2026, suggesting a coordinated research event in open computational chemistry. The peroxovanadates repo also had a README update on Sep 23, 2025.

3. **Ocean geoengineering is silent** — No ocean intervention repositories show meaningful activity. The absence itself is a governance and institutional story.

4. **Legacy Fortran dominates** — AM3 (2015, 4 commits total) and PMIP analyzers are Fortran-based. The modern Python/data-science shift hasn't reached geoengineering-specific simulation.

5. **Dormancy is the default** — 10 of 14 discovered repos show no activity in the last year. Climate tech open source is fragile.

6. **OpenAir-Cyan matured in a single burst** — 6 commits on Feb 12, 2024 (OSHWA certification, CITATION.cff, README), then complete silence. Last activity: Feb 2024.

7. **Open Sustainable Technology is the most actively maintained** — 10 commits in the last 3 months (Jul-Sep 2026), with multiple contributors. This is the directory project, not a simulation tool, but its sustained activity suggests growing community interest.

8. **OpenAir-Sorbent-Tester is also dormant** — Last meaningful commit Oct 2023 (materials list update). Built on the Cyan platform but hasn't had activity in over 2 years.
