## 🚨 Headline Finding: The Ocean Intervention Gap

Our GitHub search across multiple query strategies returned **zero dedicated ocean geoengineering repositories.** This is the most significant finding of the entire research effort. Ocean geoengineering is the "dark matter" of climate tech on GitHub — it exists in the scientific literature but not in open code.

**See:** [ocean-intervention/COMMIT-TRENDS-OCEAN.md](https://github.com/bro26man-hash/climate-pod-research/blob/ocean-intervention/COMMIT-TRENDS-OCEAN.md) and [ocean-intervention/OCEAN-GAP-ANALYSIS.md](https://github.com/bro26man-hash/climate-pod-research/blob/ocean-intervention/OCEAN-GAP-ANALYSIS.md)

## 📊 Commit Trend Highlights (Updated Sep 2026)

### What the commit histories tell us:

1. **Institutional bursts dominate climate software** — PCMDI's 133★ had 10 commits in 2 days (v4.2.1 release, Sep 3-4, 2026). WRF's 1,761★ had v4.8.0 release in Jun 2026. MDTF-diagnostics had 10 commits Jun-Aug 2026. All institutional, all funded.

2. **The August 2026 DAC materials wave** — Four repositories (peroxovanadates, peroxotitanates, electro-swing DAC, ChemicalEngineeringAI/Carbon-Capture) were all updated around Aug 19-23, 2026, suggesting a coordinated research event in open computational chemistry.

3. **Ocean geoengineering is silent** — No ocean intervention repositories show meaningful activity. The absence itself is a governance and institutional story.

4. **Legacy Fortran dominates** — AM3 (2015, 4 commits total) and PMIP analyzers are Fortran-based. The modern Python/data-science shift hasn't reached geoengineering-specific simulation.

5. **MDTF-diagnostics is the ocean-adjacent tool to watch** — Its precipitation-buoyancy POD (Jun 2026) is the closest thing to ocean process diagnostics in open source.

6. **Dormancy is the default** — 15 of 18 repos show no meaningful activity in the last year. Climate tech open source is fragile.

7. **ClimateMARGO's revival** — Two README updates in Aug 2026 after 2+ years dormant. Could signal growing policy-modeling interest, or could be another false start.

8. **carbon-capture-and-storage is the ghost project** — 85 stars, zero activity since May 2021. Academic incentive structures don't reward long-term code stewardship.
