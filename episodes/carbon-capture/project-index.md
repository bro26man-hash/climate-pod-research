## 📈 Commit Trend Takeaway

Two distinct rhythms dominate the open DAC/capture landscape:

1. **Computational materials-science (frequent, narrow):** `DAC_peroxovanadates` and `DAC_peroxotitanates` (tjz21) both refreshed in Aug 2026 with single-purpose computational supplementary information tied to peer-reviewed studies (DOIs: 10.1039/D3SC05381D; 10.1021/acs.chemmater.4c01795). High chemistry density, low sustainability — each repo live for weeks then quiet.
2. **System engineering / planning (bursty):** `OpenCarbon` (terranexum) had a dense mid-2023 merge/README burst and is now dormant; `electro-swing-dacc` is a DIY hardware plans repo updated Aug 2026.

**Net trend:** Open-source DAC is dominated by one-off computational-chemistry supplements and hobbyist hardware plans, not by sustained, reusable simulation or system-integration engines. The August 2026 cluster ofDAC-materials repos hints at a small wave of open computational chemistry for next-gen sorbents — but none has yet crossed into openly maintained process-modeling or techno-economic tooling. The podcast angle: **open hardware + open equations exist; open, validated DAC *systems* do not.**
