## Commit-trend highlight
- **CESM** is the most active repo; 2026 commits center on CESM3 (alpha/beta), GPU/pelayout optimization, and CO2 compset naming — *not* SRM injection physics.
- **Insolation.jl** (CliMA) is the only actively maintained, purpose-built solar/radiative tool.
- Open SRM physics is scarce; governance, radiative-transfer, and legacy atmospheric code dominate the open landscape.
