## Detailed Profiles

### 1. openair-collective/openair-cyan — The DIY Beacon
- **Why it matters:** OpenAir-Cyan is the ONLY open-source, OSHWA-certified DIY direct air capture device. It's hardware, not just software — a working physical device that anyone can build.
- **Key commits:**
  - **Feb 12, 2024: 15 commits in a single day** — complete device documentation blitz. This is the "single-day build" pattern seen across climate tech.
  - Jul 2022–May 2022: Earlier development by DaOfficialWizard (5 commits)
  - **Zero activity since Feb 2024.** The device works, the docs are done, the community is frozen.
- **Why it's special:** OSHWA certification (US001095) means it meets open-source hardware standards. This is the only hardware project in the entire geoengineering/carbon capture space that is both open-source and certified.
- **Episode angle:** "15 commits in one day, then silence. The only DIY DAC device in the world was built in a blaze of glory and then frozen. Why did the community stop?"

### 2. zikribayrakar/Carbon_Capture_ML — The Survey Layer
- **Why it matters:** A comprehensive survey of ML applied to carbon capture — papers, code, datasets. Not a tool itself, but a map of the territory.
- **Key commits:**
  - 12 commits in 10 days (Jan 2023) — rapid survey compilation
  - Last commit: May 2024
  - No new material since 2024 — the ML-for-carbon-capture field has evolved
- **Episode angle:** "A survey of carbon capture ML papers became the most-starred carbon capture repo on GitHub. Then it stopped. The field moved faster than the bibliography."

### 3. The August 2026 Peroxide Wave — TJZ21's DAC Materials Series
- **Why it matters:** Two computational chemistry repos (peroxovanadates, peroxotitanates) by the same researcher represent a new wave of DAC sorbent research — computational screening of peroxide-based materials.
- **Key commits (DAC_peroxovanadates):**
  - Sep 12, 2025: CC0 license added (open science commitment)
  - Sep 23, 2025: README updated with paper DOI
  - Dec 2023–Mar 2024:密集 README updates (paper preparation phase)
  - **Total: 15 commits, allREADME-focused** — no simulation code, just paper documentation
- **Key commits (DAC_peroxotitanates):** Updated Aug 19, 2026 — same pattern
- **Episode angle:** "Two repos, two peroxides, zero simulation code. The DAC materials wave is computational screening, not open-source simulation. The code is in the papers."

### 4. yohanesnuwara/climate-capture-and-storage — The Ghost Project
- **Why it matters:** 85 stars (the most-starred carbon capture repo) but ZERO activity since March 2021. A reservoir simulation + geomechanics tool that was abandoned after its initial release.
- **Episode angle:** "85 stars, zero commits in 5 years. The most popular carbon capture repo on GitHub is a fossil. What does it mean when the best tool is the one nobody maintains?"

### 5. The Synthetic Instruments (CarbonSink, CarbonVault, etc.) — The Web3 Phenomenon
- **CarbonSink** (bigg-kay): "Direct air capture and carbon removal technology synthetic instrument" — a synthetic asset tracking DAC tech
- **CarbonVault** (drakemesh & aslembadru): "Carbon removal protocol tokenizing direct air capture technology" — tokenized carbon removal
- **ERIE-ATMO** (ericrenone): "Mauna-Loa Keeling-Curve 430-ppm col(F)/ker(F) of Atmospheric Carbon Reckoning" — concept art, not simulation
- **Pattern:** These are Web3/crypto climate projects. They have active maintainers but are not scientific tools. They trade in carbon not chemistry.
- **Episode angle:** "While chemists are screening peroxides, crypto people are tokenizing carbon removal. Are these the same ecosystem? Should they be?"

## Key Patterns for the Episode

1. **The single-day blitz then freeze:** OpenAir-Cyan (15 commits/1 day, then frozen) and the peroxostar repos (paper-documentation blit) share this pattern. Build fast, then leave.
2. **No simulation runs:** The peroxovanadates/peroxotitanates repos have zero simulation code. They're computational chemistry screenings, not open-source simulators.
3. **The governance vacuum:** No active governance, policy, or regulatory tools for carbon capture. The only "governance" is OSHWA certification for one DIY device.
4. **Web3 infilitration:** Multiple synthetic/tokenized carbon removal projects exist. The carbon capture space has attracted crypto attention that the solar geoengineering space has not.
5. **The ghost problem:** The most-starred repo (climate-capture-and-storage, 85 stars) has been dead for 5 years. The second-most-starred (openair-cyan, 76 stars) has been frozen for 2.5 years.
