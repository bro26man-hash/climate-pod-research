| Repo | Stars | Last Activity | What It Tells Us |

| OpenAir-Cyan | 76 | Feb 2024 | Hardware is achievable; OSHWA-certified; but code is frozen |
| Carbon_Capture_ML | 56 | May 2024 | Survey is useful; but not actively updated |
| carbon-capture-and-storage | 85 | May 2021 | High stars, zero activity — the ghost pattern |
| DAC_peroxovanadates | 2 | Aug 2026 | Active but tiny — the materials wave |
| CarbonLens | Unknown | Jun 2026 | Life-cycle assessment tool — niche but important |
| carbon-capture-cant-solve-climate-change | Unknown | Jul 2026 | ProPublica data — accountability journalism |

**The story:** Open-source carbon capture is bifurcated. On one side: hardware projects (OpenAir-Cyan) that are real but frozen. On the other: survey and list projects (Carbon_Capture_ML) that are useful but static. The complex simulation projects are dead. The new materials wave (Aug 2026) is small but active. Nobody is building the "AWSS for CC" — an open-source, continuously updated, operational-scale DAC simulation system.
