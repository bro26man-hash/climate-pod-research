## 🔥 Top 5 Findings This Update

### 1. WRF Just Fixed a Solar Radiation Bug (v4.8.0)
The atmospheric model that simulates every SRM scenario on Earth had a bug in its solar radiation calculation (`e836cd6`, May 28, 2026). The fix was part of v4.8.0, released June 8, 2026. **If you're simulating a dimmed sun, you need the code that calculates the sunlight to be right.**

### 2. PCMDI Prevented All Models from Looking Perfect (v4.2.1)
On September 4, 2026, PCMDI released v4.2.1 with a roundoff fix (`90cbc50`) that prevented mean climate figures from rounding to exactly 1.00. Without this fix, all models would appear to agree perfectly when they didn't. **The evaluation tools for geoengineering scenarios are being actively improved — right now.**

### 3. The CC0 Revolution in DAC Materials (4 total stars)
Both `DAC_peroxovanadates` and `DAC_peroxotitanates` are licensed CC0 — public domain dedication. Two researchers gave away their computational screening data with no rights reserved. **The most open climate science on GitHub has the lowest adoption.** Is open access the same as open adoption?

### 4. OpenAir-Cyan's Frozen Blitz (2+ years of silence)
6 commits on Feb 12, 2024 — all documentation. OSHWA certification obtained. CITATION.cff created. Then silence. The DIY DACC device is complete, certified, and documented. But nobody's building on it. **Open-source hardware hit a publish-or-perish wall.**

### 5. The Ocean Gap (ZERO repos)
10+ GitHub search queries. Zero results. No ocean alkalinity enhancement code. No marine cloud brightening models. No iron fertilization tools. The ocean is the "dark matter" of climate tech on GitHub. **The silence is itself a governance signal.**
