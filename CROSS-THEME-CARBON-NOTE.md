## The Carbon Capture Episode: Core Narrative

Carbon capture has the most GitHub activity of any climate tech domain. 2,552★ directories, 76★ hardware projects, 85★ ghost repos, 56★ literature surveys. But the activity pattern reveals a field in transition: from hardware breadfields to computational screening, from individual efforts to coordinated waves.

The August 2026 wave — 6 repos, 5 days, 4 different technical approaches — is the big signal. Something aligned. A shared paper? A benchmark? A community challenge? We don't know yet, but the synchronized updates across peroxovanadates, peroxotitanates, soft-sensors, membranes, and electro-swing are unprecedented.

## Three Cross-Cutting Themes

### 1. The Benchmark Moment
Carbon_Capture_ML (56★) added the OpenDAC paper in May 2024. OpenDAC = Open DAC materials dataset. When a field creates a shared benchmark, it's a signal of maturation. Like ImageNet for计算机 vision, or GLUE for NLP. The carbon capture field is having its benchmark moment.

### 2. The Hardware Illusion
OpenAir-Cyan (76★) proved open-source DAC hardware can work — and got OSHWA certified (Feb 12, 2024). But it died. 2.5 years of silence. The CITATION.cff creation shows the authors wanted credit, not stewardship. The hardware worked; the community didn't form.

### 3. The Coordination Mystery
Six repos, four approaches, five days. This isn't maintenance — it's synchronized discovery. Why would someone working on peroxovanadates and someone working on membranes update in the same week? Options: shared paper, conference deadline, community challenge, or breakthrough.

## What the Solar Theme Has in Common
Both solar and carbon capture suffer from the paper-to-tool gap. ClimateMARGO (solar) and Carbon_Capture_ML (carbon) are both literature/survey projects — they curate, they don't simulate. The WRF TEMPO staging-off (solar, May 2026) and the peroxovanadates update (carbon, Aug 2026) are both incremental improvements to existing approaches. Neither is a breakthrough.

## What the Ocean Theme Reveals by Contrast
The ocean quadrant has ZERO repos. Carbon capture has at least OpenAir-Cyan and Carbon_Capture_ML. Solar has WRF and PCMDI. Ocean has... nothing. The carbon theme shows that even a struggling field produces open-source artifacts. The ocean theme produces nothing at all.
