## Why Capture Can't Sustain
1. Hardware gap (machines, not just code)
2. Expertise bottleneck (5 domains needed)
3. Academic incentives (publish, not maintain)
4. Corporate displacement (internal tools)
5. Funding vacuum (research grants, not maintenance)

---

## Why Open-Sustainable-Technology DOES Survive
Low barrier (add a link), distributed labor, minimal maintenance, persistent value. The lesson: low-barrier models sustain; high-barrier models don't.
