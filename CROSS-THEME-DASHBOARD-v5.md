## 🔑 Headline Finding (v5)

**The Ocean Gap persists and deepens.** 12 search queries. Zero dedicated ocean geoengineering repositories. Five major ocean intervention approaches (MCB, iron fertilization, alkalinity enhancement, artificial upwelling, deep ocean storage) with exactly zero GitHub presence. Meanwhile, atmospheric models get 1,761 stars and carbon capture gets CC0 revolutionaries. The ocean is the "dark matter" of climate tech — invisible, undetected, and yet presumably massive.

**The surprising v5 find:** The most *actively maintained* solar geoengineering repo is `brandonhimpfen/awesome-geoengineering` — a curated links list with 7 commits in 14 months. The most *intellectually important* solar repo is `PSLmodels/Geo-DICE` — the DICE model with geoengineering, 4 commits, 6 years dead, 2 stars. **The meta-tool outlives the simulation. The museum piece is more important than the living directory.**

**The CC0 story expands.** Two DAC materials repos dedicated their data to the public domain on Sep 12, 2025. But OpenAir-Cyan's OSHWA certification blitz (6 commits in 1 day → 2.5 years of silence) suggests that **open hardware certification may be the wrong goal**. You can certify a device and still never build it. The CC0 researchers at least said "the data is yours" and then stopped talking. OpenAir-Cyan said "we're certified!" and then stopped everything.
