## 🔥 Five Headline Findings (v4)

### 1. 💀 The CC0 License Revolution
Two DAC materials repos (`tjz21/DAC_peroxovanadates` & `tjz21/DAC_peroxotitanates`) adopted **CC0 public domain** in Sep 2025. Researchers are treating computational screening data as public infrastructure — not open-source, not public-license, but **public domain**. No copyright. No restrictions.

### 2. 🌧️ The Precipitation-Buoyancy POD Sprint
On **June 19, 2026**, NOAA-GFDL made **5 commits to one file** in a single day, adding the most ocean-relevant diagnostic in open source. It's a ruler, not a crystal ball — measures model accuracy, not interventions.

### 3. 👻 ClimateMARGO's Phantom Revival
Two README updates on Aug 17, 2026 after 2+ years of silence. Zero code commits. The academic equivalent of repainting the storefront while the lights stay off.

### 4. ⚪ srm-forever — The Zero-Star Theorist
The most conceptually important repo in our research has **zero stars**. Weitzman certainty-equivalent discounting for SRM cost dynamics. "What does it cost to keep solar radiation management running forever?" Nobody's watching.

### 5. 🌊 The Ocean Intervention Gap
**10+ query strategies. Zero repositories.** Ocean geoengineering is the "dark matter" of climate tech on GitHub — abundant in literature (50+ OAE papers, 100+ iron fertilization papers), absent from code.
