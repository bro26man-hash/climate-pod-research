## 🚨 Headline Finding: The Ocean Intervention Gap

**Exhaustive GitHub search returned ZERO ocean geoengineering repositories** across 4 distinct query strategies. Ocean geoengineering is the "dark matter" of climate tech on GitHub — present in scientific literature but absent from open code.

This is the central narrative of Episode 3. See: [ocean-intervention/Ep3-Ocean-Intervention-Research.md](https://github.com/bro26man-hash/climate-pod-research/blob/ocean-intervention/Ep3-Ocean-Intervention-Research.md) and [ocean-intervention/Ocean-Gap-Analysis.md](https://github.com/bro26man-hash/climate-pod-research/blob/ocean-intervention/Ocean-Gap-Analysis.md)

## 📊 Commit Trend Summary (Across All Themes)

| Trend | Detail | Implication |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| **Bursty development** | ClimateMARGO: 10 commits in 2 days, then 2-year gap | Research software is event-driven, not sustainable |
| **Academic abandonware** | Geo-DICE: 4 commits over 2 years, then 8 years dead | Professors build tools for papers, not communities |
| **Coordinated release events** | Aug 19, 2026: 3 DAC materials repos updated same day | Research groups may be coordinating preprint drops |
| **Community lists outlast models** | awesome-geoengineering: 7 steady commits; models: dormant | Curation is easier than computation |
| **Single-day launches** | GCCS-Core: 10 commits in one day, then silence | GitHub launch events are mirages |
| **ML compressing discovery** | MOF predictor: full pipeline in 3 days (Dec 29–30, 2025) | AI is accelerating the compute side of climate tech |
| **Ocean = silent** | Zero repos, zero commits, zero activity | The biggest governance & institutional question in the field |
