- **Pattern**: Steady maintenance of legacy models; no new wave of SRM-specific code
- **Language**: Fortran (legacy) + Python (newer tools)

### 🌍 Carbon Capture
- **Bursty + steady dual mode**: tjz21 single-commit SI pushes Aug 2026; OpenCarbon multi-file sprint Jul 2023; Cyan certifications Aug 2026
- **August 2026 surge**: 4 DAC materials repos updated in one week — computational chemistry for next-gen sorbents
- **Hardware maturation**: Cyan's OSHWA certification signals shift from hacker project to regulated device
- **Language**: Python dominates new code; hardware uses C++/KiCad

### 🌊 Ocean Intervention

- **Nearest-adjacent**: Team50-Labs/NebuGrid (1 commit, Aug 2026 — fog harvesting, not geoengineering)
- **Only API/metadata profiles**: Clairity & Spiritus (Sep 2026, no code)
