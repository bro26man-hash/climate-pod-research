## The CC0 License Trend — The Biggest Open-Science Story (Updated)

Both tjz21 repos adopted CC0 on Sep 12, 2025. This is not just "open source" — it's public domain dedication. The key observations:

1. **Same author, same day:** Jacob Hirschi added CC0 to both repos simultaneously
2. **Same content type:** Computational DAC materials screening (peroxovanadates and peroxotitanates)
3. **Same intent:** Making the data publicly infrastructure, not just open-access
4. **No other CC0 repos found in carbon capture:** This makes the tjz21 repos uniquely positioned

**Why CC0 matters for climate tech:** CC0 removes all legal friction. You can build on it, redistribute it, use it commercially, modify it — no license, no attribution required, no questions asked. This is the legal equivalent of "this data is as fundamental as the periodic table." If other researchers follow, it could create a public-domain data commons for DAC materials.

## Five Key Findings (Updated v4)

1. **Hardware projects burst then die.** OpenAir-Cyan had 6 commits in 1 day (certification), then silence. The mechanism is clear: a PhD student or researcher drives the project, publishes, moves on, and nobody maintains the repo.

2. **Literature surveys are still the dominant open-source mode.** Carbon_Capture_ML (56 stars) curates papers; it doesn't simulate anything. But the new competitor is AI-augmented simulation (differentiable-flowsheets) — which may displace the literature-survey model.

3. **Academic thesis projects are ghosts.** carbon-capture-and-storage (85 stars) has zero activity since May 2021. Stars does not equal maintenance.

4. **The AI-augmented simulation wave is the new signal.** differentiable-flowsheets has 10 commits in 5 days, with Claude authoring production code. This is not literature curation — it's live engineering with AI assistance. The August/September 2026 wave is not just about materials — it's about AI-powered process simulation.

5. **The CC0 license trend continues to be the most important structural signal.** tjz21's public domain dedication (Sep 2025) predates the AI wave. But the two trends may converge: if AI-generated designs are trained on CC0-licensed screening data, the public-domain commitment becomes foundational infrastructure for the entire AI-mentioned materials discovery pipeline.
