## Key Findings Summary

- **Zero ocean geoengineering repos** across 7 search strategies
- **Best ocean tools have zero geoengineering features** — Oceananigans, veros, OceanBioME are general-purpose
- **Julia dominates ocean simulation** — 4 of 6 adjacent repos use Julia
- **MAOOAM dormant 3 years** — the only coupled ocean-atmosphere model with zero recent commits
- **The gap is governance signal** — absence of code may reflect self-regulation by the research community
