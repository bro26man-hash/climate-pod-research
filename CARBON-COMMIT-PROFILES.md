## Tier 1 — Most Active & Impactful

### OpenAir-Cyan (openair-collective/openair-cyan)
- **Stars:** 76 | **Forks:** 5 | **Language:** C++/Hardware | **License:** CERN-OHL-P + GPL-3.0
- **Last commit:** February 12, 2024 (OSHWA certification push)
- **Key milestone:** OSHWA UID US001095 — certified open hardware DACC device
- **SRM Relevance:** Passive direct air capture using calcium hydroxide/hydrated lime. 1.5W air pump. Full assembly documentation.
- **Current status:** Dormant since certification (Feb 2024), but the most complete open-source DAC hardware project ever published
- **Why it still matters:** It's the proof-of-concept that open-source DAC hardware can work — from design to certification

- **Stars:** 2,546 | **Forks:** 300+ | **Language:** Markdown/Directory | **License:** MIT
- **Last commit:** September 9, 2026 (2 new tools added)
