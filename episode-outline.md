# 🎙️ Episode Outline — Ocean Intervention

## Segment 1: What is Ocean Geoengineering?
- Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (OAE)
- Iron fertilization
- Upwelling/downwelling manipulation
- Seaweed farming and blue carbon
- Ocean thermal energy conversion (OTEC)
- Key episode question: Why is nobody building open-source tools for this?

## Segment 2: What We Actually Found
- NebuGrid: fog harvesting as ocean-atmosphere analog
- open-sustainable-technology as umbrella discovery path
- The curious absence of dedicated ocean-geo repos

## Segment 3: The Ocean-Intervention Gap
- Why it's empty: experimental complexity, governance concerns, institutional siloing
- Compare to solar and carbon capture — why do they have code and ocean doesn't?
- What open-source ocean intervention COULD look like

## Segment 4: Promising Directions
- MITgcm as a potential open fork for geo scenarios
- Seaweed farming as the friendly ocean CDR
- OAE as the most mature but most regulation-limited approach
- Legal and governance frameworks as barriers to openness
