# 🎙️ Episode Outline — Solar Geoengineering

## Segment 1: What is Solar Geoengineering?
- Definition: SRM, albedo modification, solar radiation management
- Why it's controversial: governance, termination shock, moral hazard
- Key episode question: *Can we simulate SRM safely before we deploy it?*

## Segment 2: The Open-Source Simulation Landscape
- tour of FMS-ESM/AM3 (GFDL atmospheric model)
- open-earth-digital-twin-simulation as a potential SRM testbed
- RhondaMueller's Arctic permafrost analysis code

## Segment 3: What the Commit History Tells Us
- Sparse activity = low community engagement / high complexity
- Most development is in closed academic environments
- The few open repos are either legacy (AM3) or experimental (digital twin)

## Segment 4: Risks & Uncertainties
- Arctic-specific findings from Codes-RFG-Arctic-Impacts
- Termination shock: what happens if SRM is suddenly stopped?
- Governance gaps in simulation and deployment
