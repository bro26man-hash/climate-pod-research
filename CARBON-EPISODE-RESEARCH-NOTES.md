## The Three Stories

### 1. The Open Hardware Story (OpenAir-Cyan)
- **What:** DIY direct air capture device, OSHWA-certified open-source hardware
- **Why it matters:** First legally-recognized open-source carbon capture device
- **The twist:** 6 commits in one day (Feb 12, 2024), then 2.5 years of silence
- **Key quote:** "It went from hobby project to certified hardware in a single sprint"
- **URL:** https://github.com/openair-collective/openair-cyan

### 2. The Solo Researcher Story (Carbon_Capture_ML)
- **What:** One person's literature review of 500+ ML papers on carbon capture
- **Why it matters:** Proves that solo researchers can carry an entire field's knowledge base
- **The twist:** 5 papers in 4 days (Feb 2023), then slow decline
- **Key quote:** "This isn't a software project — it's a master's thesis being built in public"
- **URL:** https://github.com/zikribayraktar/Carbon_Capture_ML

### 3. The Revolution Story (CC0 + DAE-LSTM)
- **What:** CC0 licensing for DAC materials data + hybrid physics-ML modeling
- **Why it matters:** The future of open science and industrial carbon capture
- **The twist:** 2-star repos with the most radical licenses and the most sophisticated modeling
- **Key quote:** "Two stars, CC0 license, and the most radical idea in carbon capture"
- **URLs:** 
  - https://github.com/tjz21/DAC_peroxovanadates (CC0)
  - https://github.com/tonyzyl/CO2-Soft-sensor-for-a-carbon-capture-pilot-plant (DAE-LSTM)

## Q&A Potential Questions

1. **"Is open-source carbon capture actually feasible?"**
   - OpenAir-Cyan proves it's *technically* feasible. OSHWA certification proves it's *legally* feasible. But the 2.5-year silence proves it's not *sustainably* feasible without funding.

2. **"Why do ghost repos have more stars than active ones?"
   - Citation counting ≠ usage counting. Academic repos accumulate stars from papers citing them, not from people using the code. This inflates the perceived health of the field.

3. **"Could CC0 change everything?"
   - If materials data is public domain, it becomes infrastructure. You can build on it without asking. But infrastructure is boring — until someone builds something *with* it.

4. **"Is DAE-LSTM the future?"
   - It's the most credible approach for industrial deployment. Pure ML fails on edge cases. Pure physics fails on complexity. The hybrid works because it knows what it doesn't know.
