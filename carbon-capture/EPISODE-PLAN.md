# 🌍 Episode Plan — Carbon Capture

## Working Title
"Five Hundred Dollars to Save the Planet? The Open-Source DAC Revolution"

## Episode Arc

### Act 1: The Cost Crisis
- Current DAC costs: $400-1,000/ton of CO₂
- The $1000/ton target that nobody has hit
- Why is DAC so expensive? (Energy, sorbents, engineering)

### Act 2: The Open-Source Builders
- **OpenAir-Cyan:** $500 DIY DAC device. OSHWA-certified. Real hardware. The showpiece.
- **Digital Twin (isaH93):** Python-based TVSA simulation. The next-gen modeling approach.
- **Peroxovanadate screening:** Computational DFT materials search. The future of sorbent discovery?
- **OpenSustainableTechnology (2,546★):** The index that maps the whole landscape.

### Act 3: Can Open Source Crack the Cost Problem?
- Hardware iteration speed: Can community-driven development beat corporate R&D?
- The sorbent bottleneck: Materials science is the rate-limiting step
- The simulation gap: No open-source DAC process simulator exists
- The August 2026 wave: Was there a coordinated research event?

## Key Segments
1. **"The $1,000/ton Problem"** — Why DAC costs what it does
2. **"Build It Yourself"** — OpenAir-Cyan and the DIY DAC movement
3. **"The Digital Twin"** — Python-based process simulation for DAC
4. **"Peroxides and Paper"** — Computational sorbent screening
5. **"The $500 Question"** — Can open-source hardware beat the cost target?
