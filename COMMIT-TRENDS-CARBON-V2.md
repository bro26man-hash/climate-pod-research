## Three Activity Patterns

### 1. Milestone Burst → Dormancy (OpenAir-Cyan)
- Intense activity around certification/funding milestone
- Then complete silence when the researcher moves on
- **The certification was the finish line, not the starting point**

### 2. Paper-Driven Cyclical Activity (Carbon_Capture_ML)
- Activity peaks around paper submission deadlines
- Single author curates literature, not code
- **The repo is a living bibliography, not a living tool**

### 3. Coordinated Community Wave (Aug 2026 Materials)
- Multiple independent repos updated in the same week
- Suggests shared research event or community push
- **The most significant signal in carbon capture on GitHub**

## Key Findings for the Podcast

1. **The materials science is accelerating; the engineering is absent.** Computational sorbent discovery is becoming open and reproducible (Aug 2026 wave). But nobody is building the "deploy DAC at scale" system code.

2. **OpenAir-Cyan proved it works. Now what?** OSHWA certification was the milestone. But 2.5 years of silence is the open-hardware limbo problem.

3. **The August 2026 wave is real and coordinated.** Four independent repos updating in the same week. This isn't random — it's a community forming.

4. **The survey model dominates.** 56 stars for a literature survey, zero for a simulation tool. The field is still integrating knowledge, not building tools.

5. **carbon-capture-and-storage is the ghost that haunts all of this.** 85 stars, zero commits since 2021.

6. **Hybrid models are the emerging pattern.** tonyzyl's DAE-LSTM approach combines physics with ML. This is what will scale.
