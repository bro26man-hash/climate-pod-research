## The Five Hypotheses for Why the Ocean Is Empty
1. **Computational:** 100x more grid resolution needed than atmospheric models
2. **Irreversibility:** You can't un-disperse alkalinity in the ocean
3. **Governance:** No treaty, no framework, no funding mechanism
4. **Ecological risk:** Ocean interventions are biology problems, not physics problems
5. **Community size:** Too few researchers to sustain a codebase

## The Three Universes
- 🔴 **Fast:** WRF, PCMDI, MDTF — institutional, funded, sustained
- 🟡 **Slow:** ClimateMARGO, OpenAir-Cyan — individual, unfunded, dormant
- ⚫ **Empty:** Ocean geoengineering — zero repos, zero code, zero governance
