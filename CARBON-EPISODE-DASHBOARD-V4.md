## The CC0 Question

**Is CC0 the answer to climate tech's sustainability problem?**

**PROS:**
- ✅ Removes barriers to adoption
- ✅ Enables commercial use without permission
- ✅ Creates truly public infrastructure
- ✅ Aligns with crisis urgency

**CONS:**
- ❌ No mechanism for maintenance accountability
- ❌ No attribution = no credit for researchers
- ❌ Can discourage corporate investment
- ❌ Doesn't solve the FUNDING problem

**Podcast question:** *"If CC0 means anyone can use your data forever, does it also mean nobody is responsible for keeping it updated?"*
