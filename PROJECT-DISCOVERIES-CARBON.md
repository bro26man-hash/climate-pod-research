## 🎙️ Episode Hooks (Updated v3)

1. **OpenAir-Cyan proved it can be done — and then stopped.** The OSHWA certification was the milestone (Feb 12, 2024 — 6 commits in 1 day, including CITATION.cff creation and OSHWA UID logo). But 2.5 years of silence is the open-hardware limbo problem.

2. **The August 2026 wave is the big signal.** Six repos, five days, three approaches (peroxovanadates, peroxotitanates, membranes, soft-sensors, electro-swing). This is either a paper drop, a community challenge, or a breakthrough. The podcast should investigate.

3. **The field is in knowledge-integration mode.** The dominant repo (Carbon_Capture_ML, 56 stars) curates papers. The OpenDAC paper (May 2024) is the field's shared benchmark. Nobody is building new simulation tools — they're curating and organizing existing knowledge.

4. **The ghost project haunts us.** carbon-capture-and-storage (85 stars, dormant since May 2021) is the prototype of the "stars without sustainability" problem. 10 commits in 3 months, then silence for 5.3 years.

5. **The soft-sensor is the sleeper.** A DAE-LSTM hybrid model for plant control (updated Aug 18, 2026) is more immediately deployable than any materials discovery. It's the "deploy at scale" code that's been missing.

6. **The membrane model is the surprise entry.** CCSI-Toolset's hollow fiber membrane model (updated Aug 23, 2026) shows that physical separation is also entering the open-source computational space. Membranes are a different pathway from sorbents.
