## 🕸️ The CC0 License Revolution

### The Biggest Open-Science Signal in Carbon Capture GitHub
Two DAC materials repositories adopted **CC0 (Creative Commons Zero / Public Domain Dedication)**:
- [tjz21/DAC_peroxovanadates](https://github.com/tjz21/DAC_peroxovanadates)
- [tjz21/DAC_peroxotitanates](https://github.com/tjz21/DAC_peroxotitanates)

Both committed on **September 23, 2025** — same day, same author, same license choice.

**What CC0 means:** No copyright. No restrictions. Anyone can use, modify, distribute, even commercially, without permission. The researcher is not just open-sourcing code — they're dedicating *all data, all results, all computational screening output* to public infrastructure.

**Why this matters:** Legacy carbon capture research is paywalled. Lab notebooks are proprietary. But CC0 computational screening data? That's a new paradigm. Computational chemistry results — reproducible, deterministic, no human subjects — are being released as public infrastructure.

**Episode hook v5:** *"A researcher in computational DAC materials decided that the best way to accelerate carbon capture science wasn't to publish papers — it was to give away all the data. No copyright. No paywall. Public domain."*
