## The Ocean Intervention Gap

### Why Is This the Biggest Finding?

The scientific literature is **rich** with ocean geoengineering proposals:
- **Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (OAE):** Adding lime or olivine to seawater to increase CO₂ absorption
- **Iron Fertilization:** Adding iron to the ocean to stimulate phytoplankton blooms, sequestering carbon
- **Seaweed/Kelp Cultivation:** Growing marine biomass for carbon sequestration
- **Ocean Cooling:** Upwelling deep cold water to reduce sea surface temperatures (and protect coral)

But the **open-source code base is essentially empty.** Zero repositories. Zero simulation tools. Zero datasets.

### What This Tells Us

1. **Governance fear** — Ocean intervention is the most geopolitically sensitive climate tech. The Law of the Sea, London Protocol, and CBD decisions have created a chilling effect on public-facing open-source work.

2. **Risk aversion** — Unlike solar geoengineering (which has CMIP experiments) or carbon capture (which has industrial players), ocean geoengineering has no major institutional champion pushing code.

3. **Detection avoidance hypothesis** — Some researchers suggest that demonstrating ocean intervention techniques through open-source code could trigger premature or reckless deployment. The "don't build the match" argument applies especially to ocean methods.

4. **The PhD bottleneck** — Ocean geoengineering research is concentrated in a handful of university groups (e.g., IASS Potsdam, University of Plymouth, GEOMAR). Their code, if it exists, lives in private labs, not on GitHub.

5. **The "dark matter" of climate tech** — Ocean geoengineering is the "dark matter" of the climate-tech GitHub ecosystem: we know it's there from the scientific literature, but it leaves no open-source trace.

## The Governance Question

### Key Legal / Governance Frameworks
1. **London Protocol / London Convention** — Regulates ocean dumping (includes OAE)
2. **CBD (Convention on Biological Diversity)** — Moratorium on ocean fertilization
3. **UN Law of the Sea** — Flag state jurisdiction over ships
4. **EU/UK regulations** — National-level rules on marine geoengineering

### The "Dark Matter" Metaphor
Just as dark matter holds the universe together but can't be directly observed, ocean geoengineering holds the climate solution space together but can't be directly seen on GitHub. The absence is itself a signal.
